A trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5: all 55 official trophies — names, descriptions and grades exactly as published, cross-checked against the Exophase and PSNProfiles listings — each with a guide written from its official description and a source line saying so. A five-stage roadmap runs story first, then contacts and strangers, medals and mayhem, collections and cash, and the 100% finish; the three trophies whose descriptions describe a one-time chance are flagged "one to watch" rather than called missable. The tracker keeps your checklist in the browser (localStorage), filters by kind, grade and search, hides unearned story trophies behind a spoiler guard, and exports or imports your progress as JSON. Static files, no build step, fully offline.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
